## ZBM2 CW Tool 3.5.5 - very slow senders

- **Slow CW (4-10 WPM) reads right.** A 5 WPM station on 7.124 printed "T T HE F L O O R": at that speed the gap between letters looks like a word break to the neural decoder. Below 10 WPM the app now places word spaces from the timing itself, so it prints "THE FLOOR".
- **THEIR SPEED down to 4 WPM** (was 6). A 5 WPM sender reads 5.0.
- The first few seconds of a very slow station can still come out letter-spaced until its speed is known.
