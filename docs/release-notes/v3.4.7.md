## ZBM2 CW Tool 3.4.7 - safety review fixes

- **License guard no longer lets you transmit blind.** If CAT is set up but not answering, the app can't see your frequency, so it now blocks transmit and says why (before, it allowed it). No CAT at all, or guard off: unchanged.
- **Tone (audio) keying:** the guard checks where the signal really is (dial + tone in USB), not just the dial.
- **60 m:** CW only on the channel centres (5.3320, 5.3480, 5.3585, 5.3730, 5.4050 MHz), General and up.
- Rig Setup's TUNE and the Setup Wizard's transmit test now go through the license guard too.
- BAND won't change band in the middle of a transmission; the band memory also follows the radio's own knob.
- Settings are saved safely (a crash mid-save can't wipe them).
- Portable (zip) copies: update opens the download page instead of installing a second copy.
