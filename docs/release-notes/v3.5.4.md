## ZBM2 CW Tool 3.5.4 - BAND starts at your privileges

- Picking a band puts you at the **bottom of your license class's CW privileges** on it, 1 kHz in (General on 40 m: 7.026; Extra: 7.001; Technician on 80 m: 3.526). On a band where your class has no CW privileges you land at the bottom to listen, and the status line says so.

Already on 3.4.1 or newer? Click the orange **NEW VERSION** button.
