## ZBM2 CW Tool 3.4.5 - steady THEIR SPEED

- **THEIR SPEED no longer jumps.** At the start of each over, a speed search over mostly band noise could read a 25 WPM station as 46, and that showed up (and set your sending speed) within half a second. Now a big speed change has to hold for 6 seconds before it's shown, and a jump right after good copy must be confirmed twice. A real new station at a different speed still takes over in about 10 seconds.
