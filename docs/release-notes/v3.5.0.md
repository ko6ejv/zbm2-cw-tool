## ZBM2 CW Tool 3.5.0 - one decoder, live band activity

**CW for people who can't copy CW yet. Putting competent operators on the net.**

### New
- **Who's on the air right now.** Live CW spots from the Reverse Beacon Network (skimmers near you): the BAND list shows how many stations are active on each band, and the **SPOTS** button lists them with call, frequency, speed and signal. Double-click one to tune to it.
- **THEIR SPEED comes from the neural decoder itself.** It times the letters it copies, so speed and text always agree (within about 3% from 12 to 40 WPM in tests, holding steady through pauses).
- **The dits and dahs over the spectrum** now follow the neural decoder's letters too, so they show up on weak signals.
- **Lighter on the uConsole:** the older backup decoder no longer runs alongside the neural one.

### Fixed
- A startup race could stop decoding entirely; the decode thread now survives any single error.
- Background jobs (band change, tune, updates) no longer touch the screen from their own threads.
- Backup speed search: finer steps, ignores noise at the edges.
- New automated screen tests run on every change.
